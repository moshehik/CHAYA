We take accepted measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

This website does not use cookies for tracking purposes or for collecting statistical/advertising data by third parties. Should such tracking tools be added in the future, this policy will be updated and your consent will be requested as required by law.

You have the right to review your personal information held by us, and request to correct or delete it if it is inaccurate or you no longer wish it to be saved. For this purpose, you can contact us at the phone number listed on the website.
